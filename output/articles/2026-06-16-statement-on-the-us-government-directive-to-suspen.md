## What happened

The US government has issued an export control directive requiring Anthropic to suspend access to its Fable 5 and Mythos 5 AI models for foreign nationals, according to [a statement from Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/fable-mythos-access). The sweeping measure applies universally—affecting international users both within US borders and globally—and represents one of the most restrictive AI access controls issued to date.

This directive reflects growing government concern over advanced AI technology proliferation, particularly regarding models capable of sophisticated reasoning and reasoning-based tasks. The move signals a shift toward treating frontier AI systems similarly to sensitive military or telecommunications technologies under existing export regimes.

The impact extends beyond individual users. International teams at companies, research institutions, and universities face immediate compliance challenges. For Anthropic specifically, the restriction affects the accessibility of two of its most advanced models, potentially limiting collaborative research and international product development initiatives.

The company has stated it will comply fully with the directive, though specific implementation timelines and technical mechanisms for enforcement remain unclear.
