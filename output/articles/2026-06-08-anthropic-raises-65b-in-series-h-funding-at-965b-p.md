## What happened

Anthropic announced a landmark $65 billion Series H funding round, catapulting the AI safety-focused company to a nearly $1 trillion post-money valuation. The round was led by four major investment firms: Altimeter Capital, Dragoneer, Greenoaks, and Sequoia Capital, demonstrating sustained institutional appetite for AI infrastructure plays despite regulatory uncertainties.

The funding injection represents a 93% increase from Anthropic's previous Series G valuation of approximately $500 billion, achieved just months earlier. This acceleration reflects intensifying competition in the large language model space and the substantial capital requirements needed to train cutting-edge AI systems at scale.

Anthropic, founded in 2021 by former OpenAI researchers Dario and Daniela Amodei, has positioned itself as a counterweight to OpenAI through its emphasis on AI safety and constitutional AI principles. The company's flagship Claude model has gained significant traction in enterprise and consumer markets, competing directly with ChatGPT and other foundation models.

The capital infusion will likely fund expanded compute infrastructure, continued research into AI alignment and safety, and acceleration of Claude's development roadmap.
