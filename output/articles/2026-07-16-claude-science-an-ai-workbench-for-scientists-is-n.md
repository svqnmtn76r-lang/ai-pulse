## What happened

Anthropic announced the launch of Claude Science, a customizable workbench purpose-built for research applications. Rather than forcing scientists to work within a generic chatbot interface, the platform consolidates the tools, packages, and computational resources researchers rely on daily into a unified environment.

The offering addresses a critical gap in current AI deployment: while large language models have proven valuable for scientific work, existing interfaces weren't designed with research workflows in mind. Claude Science takes a different approach by maintaining auditability—a essential feature for reproducible science—while allowing researchers to flexibly allocate computing resources based on project demands.

The customizable nature of the workbench suggests scientists can tailor the environment to their specific disciplines and methodologies, whether that involves computational chemistry, bioinformatics, materials science, or theoretical research. This flexibility contrasts sharply with one-size-fits-all AI products that dominate the market.

The announcement signals Anthropic's strategic pivot toward vertical-specific AI applications. Rather than competing solely in the consumer and general-purpose enterprise space, the company is building domain-specialized tools that integrate deeply with existing research infrastructure.
