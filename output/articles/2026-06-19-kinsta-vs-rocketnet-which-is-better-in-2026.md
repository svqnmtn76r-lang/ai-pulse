## Key differences explained

**Hosting vs. optimization layer**

Kinsta provides complete managed WordPress hosting, meaning your entire infrastructure—servers, security, backups, and performance—is handled by their team. You deploy your WordPress site and Kinsta manages the rest. Rocket.net, by contrast, operates as a performance optimization layer, typically deployed on existing WordPress installations to improve caching and load times.

**Performance approach**

Kinsta bakes performance into its hosting architecture: premium servers, global CDN integration, automatic updates, and security scanning come standard. Performance optimization is systematic and managed by infrastructure professionals.

Rocket.net takes a different angle: it optimizes performance through WordPress plugins and caching strategies. This approach works well if you already have hosting elsewhere and want to squeeze more speed from your existing setup, but it requires manual configuration and ongoing tuning.

**Support and maintenance**

Kinsta includes comprehensive managed support—server updates, security patches, backups, and performance monitoring happen automatically. Your team focuses on WordPress content and functionality, not infrastructure.

Rocket.net's support is primarily documentation-driven, with users responsible for plugin configuration and maintenance. This gives you more control but requires more technical involvement.

**Cost structure**

Kinsta operates on a subscription model covering the entire hosting stack. Rocket.net pricing depends on its specific product offering—whether you're purchasing caching plugins, CDN services, or optimization bundles. Each approach suits different budgets and scaling needs; consult their official pricing pages for current details.
