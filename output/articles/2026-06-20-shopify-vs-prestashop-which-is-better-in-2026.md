# Shopify vs PrestaShop: Which is better in 2026?

**Quick answer:** Shopify is a hosted, all-in-one platform ideal for businesses seeking simplicity and support, while PrestaShop is a self-hosted solution that offers greater customization control for technical users willing to manage their own infrastructure.

## Overview

The choice between Shopify and PrestaShop remains one of the most fundamental decisions for e-commerce entrepreneurs. Both platforms have evolved significantly, yet they represent fundamentally different philosophies: Shopify emphasizes managed simplicity and scalability, while PrestaShop prioritizes flexibility and self-determination. Understanding their core differences helps businesses select the right foundation for their online store.

This comparison matters because the platform you choose affects everything from operational costs to customization capabilities, payment processing options, and long-term scalability. For 2026, both platforms continue to serve different market segments effectively, but your specific needs—not blanket recommendations—should drive the decision.

## Key differences explained

**Shopify** operates on a subscription model where the platform handles hosting, security patches, updates, and server infrastructure. This approach eliminates technical overhead but requires ongoing monthly fees. Shopify's strength lies in its curated app marketplace and professional support team, making it accessible to non-technical founders. The platform scales automatically—whether you're processing 10 or 10,000 orders daily, Shopify manages the backend complexity.

**PrestaShop**, being open-source and self-hosted, requires you to secure your own web hosting and manage server administration. This introduces complexity but provides granular control over every aspect of your store's code, database, and architecture. PrestaShop appeals to developers and businesses with specific customization requirements that Shopify's modular approach might not accommodate.

## Pricing and operational costs

Shopify's transparent subscription model makes budgeting straightforward, though transaction fees and app costs accumulate over time. PrestaShop itself is free, but realistic total costs include hosting (monthly), security certificates, backups, and potentially developer time for customization and maintenance. For small stores, Shopify often proves more cost-effective; for high-volume operations, PrestaShop's self-hosted model might offer better economics.

## Use cases

Choose **Shopify** if you prioritize speed to market, want professional support, prefer a mobile-first platform, or lack technical infrastructure expertise. Choose **PrestaShop** if you require deep customization, already manage servers, want complete ownership of your codebase, or need to optimize costs at significant scale.

## What happens next

Evaluate your team's technical capacity, expected transaction volume, and budget constraints. Test both platforms' free trials or community editions. Review their official documentation at shopify.com and prestashop.com for current features and pricing before deciding.
