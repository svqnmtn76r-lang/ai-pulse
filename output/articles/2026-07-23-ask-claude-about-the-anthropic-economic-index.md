## Key technical advantages

The connector leverages Claude's natural language processing to translate conversational questions into data queries. Users can ask economic questions like "What's the trend in unemployment rates over the last five years?" without learning database syntax. Claude interprets intent and returns both raw data and analytical summaries.

This approach also reduces cognitive load. Rather than mentally translating between systems, analysts stay in a single interface. The AI can contextualize findings, compare datasets, and identify anomalies—tasks that normally require manual inspection or additional tools.
