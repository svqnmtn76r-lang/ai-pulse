# Liquid Web vs Cloudways: managed hosting for growing sites

**Quick answer:** Liquid Web offers dedicated and cloud hosting with hands-on managed support, while Cloudways provides a more developer-friendly, self-service cloud platform built on top of major cloud providers.

## Overview

As websites grow beyond shared hosting, teams face a critical choice: opt for traditional managed hosting with dedicated infrastructure, or embrace cloud-based solutions that offer flexibility and scalability. Liquid Web and Cloudways represent two distinct approaches to this problem, each catering to different business needs and technical preferences.

Liquid Web has positioned itself as a premium managed hosting provider, emphasizing white-glove support and dedicated resources for businesses that want infrastructure managed by experts. Cloudways, by contrast, abstracts away the complexity of cloud providers like AWS, Google Cloud, and DigitalOcean, presenting a more accessible interface for developers and agencies who prefer self-service control with managed support as a secondary benefit.

Understanding the differences between these platforms matters because your choice will affect not just monthly costs, but also how much time your team spends managing infrastructure versus focusing on application development.

## Key differences explained

**Infrastructure and control:** Liquid Web manages your infrastructure end-to-end, which appeals to teams that want experts handling server decisions. You specify what you need, and Liquid Web provisions and maintains it. Cloudways flips this—you control the configuration through a dashboard, choosing CPU, RAM, and storage while Cloudways handles the underlying cloud connection.

**Support philosophy:** Liquid Web's support model centers on proactive management and dedicated account specialists. This hands-on approach suits businesses that view hosting as a service relationship. Cloudways emphasizes knowledge base resources and ticket support, making it better suited for technically confident teams.

**Scaling approach:** As your traffic grows, scaling on Liquid Web typically means upgrading to a higher plan, which can involve some transition. Cloudways allows one-click vertical scaling and better supports horizontal scaling through load balancers, though this requires more technical knowledge.

**Cost transparency:** Liquid Web publishes clear, fixed pricing tiers. Cloudways' pricing depends partly on which underlying cloud provider you choose, introducing more variables. For predictable budgets, Liquid Web's structure is simpler.

## What happens next

If your priority is **hands-off management and premium support**, explore Liquid Web's dedicated and cloud hosting plans. If you value **developer flexibility and multi-cloud optionality**, Cloudways' dashboard-driven approach may suit you better.

Compare both platforms' current pricing and feature details on their official websites, request demos, and consider running test deployments to see which control model feels right for your team's workflow.
