## What happened

Anthropic announced a massive $65 billion Series H funding round, catapulting the AI safety-focused startup to a $965 billion post-money valuation. This financing represents a significant escalation in capital deployment for the AI sector, underscoring investor appetite for alternatives to established players like OpenAI. Founded in 2021 by former OpenAI members including Dario and Daniela Amodei, Anthropic has built Claude, a large language model designed with constitutional AI principles emphasizing safety and alignment.

The funding milestone reflects growing market validation for the startup's approach to responsible AI development. With this capital infusion, Anthropic gains substantial resources to advance Claude's reasoning capabilities, expand infrastructure, and increase hiring across research and engineering teams.

The valuation jump also signals investor confidence in Anthropic's commercial prospects. The company has been expanding Claude's accessibility through APIs and enterprise partnerships, competing directly with OpenAI's GPT models and Google's Gemini in enterprise and consumer markets.
