## What happened

The US government has issued a sweeping export control directive requiring Anthropic to suspend all access to its Fable 5 and Mythos 5 models for foreign nationals, regardless of location. [According to Anthropic's statement](https://www.anthropic.com/news/fable-mythos-access), the restriction applies universally to non-US citizens, creating one of the most expansive AI model access limitations to date.

This directive represents a significant shift in how the US government is managing advanced AI technology distribution. Rather than implementing graduated restrictions based on country-of-origin or security risk assessments, the measure imposes blanket prohibitions on an entire class of users.

The move reflects escalating government concerns about AI technology proliferation and national security, following months of debate over appropriate safeguards for frontier AI systems. Fable 5 and Mythos 5 represent sophisticated capabilities that the US government apparently considers sensitive enough to warrant export-level controls typically reserved for military or dual-use technologies.

For Anthropic, compliance is immediate and non-negotiable. The company has implemented technical measures to enforce the restrictions, affecting research partnerships, international collaborations, and commercial relationships involving foreign nationals—a substantial portion of the global AI research community.

## What happens next

Organizations working with Anthropic on projects involving these models face immediate operational challenges. International research institutions may need to restructure collaborations or seek alternative platforms. The precedent established here could influence how other AI developers approach regulatory compliance and international access policies moving forward.
