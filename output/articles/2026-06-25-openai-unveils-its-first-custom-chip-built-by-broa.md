## What happened

OpenAI announced the launch of its first custom-designed semiconductor, developed in collaboration with Broadcom, according to reporting from TechCrunch. The chip represents a strategic pivot for the organization, which has historically relied on NVIDIA's GPUs for both training and inference operations.

The custom silicon is engineered to optimize inference workloads—the computationally intensive process of running trained AI models to generate outputs. By building hardware specifically tailored to its software stack, OpenAI aims to reduce operational costs while improving performance characteristics critical to serving millions of ChatGPT users.

The partnership with Broadcom, a leader in semiconductor design, positions OpenAI alongside other major AI firms investing in proprietary hardware. Google's Tensor Processing Units and Meta's custom chips have demonstrated the efficiency gains possible when hardware and software architectures align seamlessly.
