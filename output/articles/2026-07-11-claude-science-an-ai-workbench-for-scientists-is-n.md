## What happened

Anthropic has unveiled Claude Science, a purpose-built application environment tailored for research scientists and technical teams. Rather than forcing researchers to adopt generic AI chatbots, the workbench integrates commonly used scientific software packages, analysis tools, and computing resources into a unified interface.

The platform addresses a persistent gap in AI tooling: while large language models have demonstrated utility in scientific contexts, researchers often struggle integrating them into existing workflows. Claude Science solves this by embedding access to Claude—Anthropic's flagship AI model—directly within a customizable workspace that mirrors how scientists already work.

A defining feature is its emphasis on auditability. The workbench generates traceable artifacts for analyses and computations, enabling other researchers to verify results and reproduce findings. This transparency is fundamental to scientific integrity and peer review processes.

The announcement comes as AI adoption across academic and industrial research accelerates. Competitors like OpenAI and Google have similarly invested in research-focused AI products, but Claude Science's emphasis on customization and artifact transparency suggests Anthropic is positioning itself as the enterprise-grade option for institutions prioritizing reproducibility.

The platform's flexible access to computing resources—without requiring users to manage cloud infrastructure directly—particularly benefits smaller labs and academic groups with limited computational expertise. This democratizes AI-assisted research while maintaining the technical rigor the scientific community demands.
