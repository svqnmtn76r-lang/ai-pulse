## What happened

Anthropic, the San Francisco-based AI safety company behind Claude, is reportedly in talks to acquire Decart, a specialized AI startup focused on world model development, according to reporting by Bloomberg. The proposed transaction values Decart at approximately $6 billion, marking a substantial investment in frontier AI research.

World models—AI systems that can build and reason about internal representations of environments—represent a significant frontier in AI development. These technologies are considered foundational for advancing autonomous systems, robotics, and more capable AI agents that must understand spatial relationships and predict future states.

The acquisition would consolidate Anthropic's technical stack at a critical moment in the AI industry. While Anthropic has built substantial capabilities in large language models with Claude, the addition of Decart's world model expertise would address gaps in environmental reasoning and embodied AI understanding.

The company, founded in 2021 by former OpenAI and DeepMind researchers, has maintained a focused approach to AI development with emphasis on safety and interpretability. This acquisition aligns with that strategy while expanding into adjacent technical domains increasingly important for competitive differentiation.

The talks were first reported on Hacker News via Bloomberg, though official confirmation from either company remains pending. The valuation reflects broader market dynamics where specialized AI research capabilities command premium prices as the industry races toward more general and capable systems.

Watch for official announcements from both organizations regarding deal completion, regulatory review timelines, and integration plans.
