# Claude Partner Network Services Track vs. Partner Hub: What's the difference?

Quick answer: The Services Track connects enterprises with implementation specialists, while the Partner Hub offers a searchable directory and centralized resources for navigating Anthropic's partner ecosystem.

## Overview

In March 2024, Anthropic rolled out the Claude Partner Network to address a growing need in the AI deployment space: helping organizations bridge the gap between Claude's capabilities and real-world production implementations. The announcement of two new components—the Services Track and Partner Hub—represents Anthropic's effort to reduce friction in the enterprise adoption journey. For customers evaluating how to integrate Claude into their workflows, understanding these two offerings is essential to determining which resources best serve their deployment needs.

The distinction matters because enterprises face different challenges when productionizing AI. Some organizations need hands-on expertise to architect solutions, while others simply need visibility into which partners can help them. These new components address both requirements simultaneously.

## Key differences explained

**Services Track** functions as a curated marketplace for enterprises serious about production deployment. It connects organizations with Anthropic-vetted implementation partners who can handle complex integration scenarios, from architecture design through go-live support. This is ideal for teams lacking internal AI expertise or facing tight deadlines.

**Partner Hub** operates as a self-service discovery platform. It allows prospective customers to explore available partners, understand their specializations, and access shared resources like case studies and technical documentation. This approach suits organizations still evaluating whether to adopt Claude or researching different implementation approaches.

The practical implication: a startup might use Partner Hub to understand available options before committing resources, while an enterprise with a clear Claude strategy would leverage Services Track to rapidly onboard implementation support.

## Context and significance

The Partner Network announcement reflects broader trends in enterprise AI adoption. As Claude gains traction in production environments, the bottleneck shifts from AI capability to integration complexity. Organizations like Accenture, Deloitte, and specialized consultancies can now formalize their Claude expertise through these channels, creating clearer pathways for customers seeking professional services.

Anthropic's decision to split discovery (Partner Hub) from engagement (Services Track) acknowledges different customer maturity levels. Early-stage evaluators need low-friction access to information, while committed enterprises benefit from structured partnership matching.

## What happens next

Organizations implementing Claude should first evaluate whether internal resources suffice. If external expertise is needed, Partner Hub provides initial visibility into options. For production-critical deployments or complex integration scenarios, the Services Track offers a more structured engagement pathway. As the Claude Partner Network matures, expect to see increased specialization among partners—some focusing on specific industries or use cases—creating more granular matching possibilities within both tracks.
