## Key differences explained

**Accessibility**: Traditional economic tools like Bloomberg Terminal, FRED (Federal Reserve Economic Data), or Refinitiv demand either programming knowledge or expensive subscriptions. Claude's connector operates through natural language—users simply ask questions. This lowers barriers for journalists, policy analysts, educators, and business professionals without technical backgrounds.

**Workflow integration**: Rather than context-switching between a data platform and an analysis tool, everything happens within Claude. You can ask follow-up questions, request deeper analysis, or explore tangential data points without leaving the conversation.

**Instant context**: Claude can synthesize economic data with broader knowledge, explaining *why* figures matter or how they connect to current events—something raw data platforms can't do without human interpretation.

**Limitations**: The connector is specialized—it serves the Anthropic Economic Index specifically. Traditional tools offer broader data coverage, multiple data sources, and sophisticated visualization capabilities that remain superior for complex quantitative analysis or publication-ready charts.
