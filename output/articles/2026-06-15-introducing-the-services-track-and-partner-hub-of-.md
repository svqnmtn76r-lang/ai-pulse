# Claude Partner Network Services Track vs Partner Hub: What's the difference?

Quick answer: The Services Track connects customers with implementation partners, while the Partner Hub provides a centralized directory and resources for all ecosystem participants.

## Overview

Anthropic's Claude Partner Network represents a strategic expansion of how enterprises can adopt Claude in production environments. Since launching the program in March, the company recognized that customers needed better ways to discover qualified partners and navigate the growing ecosystem. The introduction of the Services Track and Partner Hub addresses this friction point, creating distinct pathways for implementation support and partner visibility. This dual-component approach reflects a maturing go-to-market strategy that recognizes different customer needs—some require hands-on deployment assistance, while others need transparent access to the full partner landscape.

## Key differences explained

**Services Track approach**: This component functions as a qualified partner marketplace specifically designed for enterprises planning Claude deployments. By curating and verifying implementation partners, Anthropic reduces procurement risk for customers and ensures partners meet specific competency standards. This track is particularly valuable for organizations that lack internal Claude expertise or have complex integration requirements. The vetting process acts as quality assurance, allowing customers to confidently select partners without extensive due diligence.

**Partner Hub advantage**: The Partner Hub operates as a transparent, accessible directory that democratizes information about the entire partner ecosystem. Rather than a gated experience, it allows prospective customers and partners to explore capabilities, review partner specialties, and access educational resources independently. This self-service model scales Anthropic's ability to support ecosystem growth without creating bottlenecks in partner discovery.

## Practical implications

For enterprises, this dual structure eliminates common adoption friction. Companies needing hands-on support can leverage the Services Track's curated recommendations, while those preferring to evaluate options independently can browse the Partner Hub. For partners, the framework provides visibility opportunities—Services Track inclusion signals competency to enterprise buyers, while Partner Hub presence ensures discoverability regardless of service model.

The separation also allows Anthropic to manage different partner categories. Consulting firms, systems integrators, and specialized Claude implementation shops can coexist in the Partner Hub while maintaining higher barriers for Services Track inclusion, which typically requires deeper platform expertise and proven deployment experience.

## What happens next

As Claude adoption accelerates across enterprises, expect these components to become the primary discovery mechanism for implementation support. Anthropic will likely expand Services Track criteria as the market matures, potentially introducing specialization tiers (healthcare, finance, legal, etc.). The Partner Hub should evolve to include more detailed case studies, benchmark data, and integration examples that help customers understand what successful Claude implementations look like across different industries.

Organizations evaluating Claude for production use should expect to interact with both components—the Hub for initial research and the Services Track for qualified partner recommendations if implementation support is needed.
