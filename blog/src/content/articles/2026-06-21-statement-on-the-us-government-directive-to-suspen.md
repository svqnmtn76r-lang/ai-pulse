- **Point 1**: The U.S. government has imposed export control restrictions blocking all foreign nationals from accessing Anthropic's Fable 5 and Mythos 5 models, effective immediately
- **Point 2**: The directive represents a significant escalation in AI model restrictions and signals growing government scrutiny of advanced AI capabilities

## What happened

Anthropic announced that it has received a directive from the U.S. government requiring the immediate suspension of access to its Fable 5 and Mythos 5 models for all foreign nationals, both domestically and internationally. The export control order, disclosed in [a statement on Anthropic's website](https://www.anthropic.com/news/fable-mythos-access), represents one of the most restrictive AI access limitations imposed on a major AI developer to date.

The restriction marks a pivotal moment in AI governance, signaling that policymakers view certain advanced model capabilities as sensitive technology subject to export controls similar to those governing semiconductors and military systems. The directive requires Anthropic to implement technical and administrative controls to verify the citizenship or residency status of users attempting to access these models.

The move impacts Anthropic's global operations significantly. The company must now enforce geolocation and identity verification mechanisms across its platforms, creating operational complexity and limiting the international reach of its product offerings. For international research institutions, non-U.S. enterprises, and users abroad, access to these specific models becomes unavailable effective immediately.

This development reflects broader tensions between advancing AI development and national security concerns. It suggests the U.S. government considers Fable 5 and Mythos 5 capabilities—their technical specifications remain undisclosed in the statement—critical enough to warrant export restrictions comparable to dual-use technologies.

## What happens next

Anthropic will need to communicate implementation timelines to affected users and adjust its product strategy accordingly. The precedent may influence how other AI developers approach model deployment and international availability going forward. Industry observers expect this could trigger similar directives targeting other advanced AI systems in coming months.
