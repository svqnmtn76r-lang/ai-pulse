## Key advantages of the connector approach

Claude's Economic Index connector eliminates friction in data exploration. Users can ask follow-up questions, request comparisons across time periods, and explore hypotheticals without switching between applications. The conversational interface makes economic data accessible to non-specialists—a product manager can query inflation trends as easily as an economist.

The connector also contextualizes raw numbers. Where traditional tools show a 3.2% unemployment rate, Claude can explain implications, historical comparisons, and related economic factors in a single response.

These tools also maintain institutional credibility and regulatory compliance that some enterprises require.

For routine queries, trend spotting, and data discovery, however, the connector's conversational approach offers efficiency gains that justify the shift.
