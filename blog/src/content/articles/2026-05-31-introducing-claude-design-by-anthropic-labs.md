## What happened

Anthropic announced the launch of Claude Design today, a specialized tool that transforms how users approach visual content creation by integrating Claude's AI capabilities into the design process. Rather than treating design as a separate workflow, users can now collaborate directly with Claude to iterate on and refine visual work—from initial concept through polished final deliverables.

The platform enables creators to generate designs, interactive prototypes, presentation decks, one-pagers, and other marketing collateral through natural conversation. Users describe what they want to build, and Claude assists in translating concepts into visual outputs, handling both creative direction and technical execution.

This launch represents a meaningful expansion of Anthropic's product ecosystem. While Claude has dominated conversational AI since its public release, Claude Design addresses a critical gap: the absence of deep visual creation capabilities within mainstream AI assistants. The move positions Anthropic to capture design professionals and product teams currently relying on multiple disconnected tools.

Released under the Anthropic Labs banner—the company's incubator for experimental features—Claude Design allows the team to gather real-world feedback before a potential wider rollout. The timing coincides with intensifying competition in AI-assisted design, where both established software providers and emerging startups are rapidly integrating generative capabilities.

The announcement underscores Anthropic's strategy to expand Claude's utility beyond text-based tasks, particularly as enterprises seek unified platforms that reduce tool fragmentation and accelerate creative workflows.

## What happens next

Users can access Claude Design immediately through Anthropic Labs. Watch for expanded capabilities and potential integration with existing Claude products as the feature moves out of experimental status.
