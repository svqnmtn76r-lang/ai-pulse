## Key differences explained

**Voice synthesis and naturalness:** ElevenLabs has built its reputation on voice quality. The platform uses proprietary text-to-speech technology designed to produce natural, expressive speech across multiple languages. Descript's voiceover features are competent but are secondary to its editing capabilities—they're included to serve creators who want to add voiceover to edited content.

**Multilingual capabilities:** This is where ElevenLabs pulls ahead. The platform supports 29+ languages with options for different accents and voice characteristics. Descript's multilingual support exists but is more limited, making ElevenLabs the clearer choice for creators working in non-English markets or producing content for global audiences.
