## What happened

Anthropic announced the launch of Claude Science, a specialized AI application tailored for the research community. Rather than forcing scientists to adapt general-purpose AI tools to their work, Claude Science integrates the computational frameworks and packages researchers already rely on daily.

The platform's core innovation centers on auditability and reproducibility. By generating documented artifacts that researchers can inspect and verify, Claude Science addresses a persistent pain point in computational science: the difficulty of tracking AI-assisted analysis steps and ensuring results can be independently validated. This becomes critical as AI increasingly influences research outcomes across disciplines.

The customizable workbench approach means scientists aren't locked into predefined workflows. Teams can configure the environment to match their specific methodologies, whether that involves particular Python libraries, statistical packages, or domain-specific tools. The platform also abstracts away computing resource management, allowing researchers to focus on their science rather than infrastructure logistics.

This launch positions Anthropic directly in the research infrastructure market, competing against both traditional scientific software vendors and emerging AI-native research tools.
