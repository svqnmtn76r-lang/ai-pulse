## What happened

Anthropic announced a sweeping government directive mandating the suspension of access to its Fable 5 and Mythos 5 AI models for foreign nationals, both domestically and internationally. [The announcement came via Anthropic's official news channel,](https://www.anthropic.com/news/fable-mythos-access) marking a turning point in how US authorities are approaching advanced AI technology distribution.

The restriction applies universally—foreign nationals cannot access these models regardless of their physical location or institutional affiliation. This represents one of the most restrictive AI export control measures issued to date, extending beyond typical geographic restrictions to encompass individual user status.

The directive reflects heightened US government concerns about advanced AI capabilities potentially reaching foreign entities or nationals without proper oversight. Fable 5 and Mythos 5 appear to represent a generation of particularly capable models that authorities deem sensitive enough to warrant comprehensive access restrictions.

For Anthropic, the directive requires immediate implementation of authentication and verification systems to ensure compliance. The company must audit existing access grants, revoke foreign national permissions, and establish ongoing monitoring mechanisms. This compliance burden extends across all deployment channels—API access, cloud instances, and direct licensing arrangements.

The move signals accelerating government intervention in AI model deployment decisions, shifting responsibility to companies for enforcement and verification. It raises questions about how similar restrictions might apply to other advanced models from competing organizations.

## What happens next

Organizations currently using or planning to integrate Fable 5 and Mythos 5 need to immediately audit user access. Anthropic will likely publish detailed compliance guidance and technical implementation frameworks to help customers identify and manage foreign national access across their systems.
