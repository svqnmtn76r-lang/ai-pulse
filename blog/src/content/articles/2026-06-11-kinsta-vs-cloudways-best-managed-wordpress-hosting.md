## Kinsta vs Cloudways: What's the difference?

**Quick answer:** Both Kinsta and Cloudways offer managed WordPress hosting optimized for performance, but Kinsta emphasizes premium infrastructure and support, while Cloudways provides more flexible, scalable cloud-based solutions at various price points.

## Overview

Choosing a managed WordPress host is a critical decision for site owners prioritizing speed and reliability. Kinsta and Cloudways both occupy the premium-to-mid-tier managed hosting space, yet they take different architectural approaches. Kinsta operates its own global data center infrastructure, while Cloudways acts as a managed layer atop popular cloud providers like DigitalOcean, Linode, and AWS. Understanding these differences helps developers and agencies select the platform that best matches their technical needs and budget constraints.

## Key differences

**Infrastructure and control**

Kinsta runs its entire operation on Google Cloud infrastructure with custom-built tools designed specifically for WordPress. This vertical integration means Kinsta controls the full stack—from server configuration to security patches. Cloudways, by contrast, provides a managed interface on top of established cloud providers. This approach gives Cloudways users the flexibility to choose their underlying infrastructure provider and potentially negotiate custom arrangements.

**Pricing and scalability**

Cloudways typically offers lower entry-level pricing, making it attractive for smaller projects or developers testing platforms. Kinsta positions itself at a premium tier with higher base costs but argues for value through included features like unlimited staging environments and advanced caching mechanisms. Both platforms scale with your needs, though Kinsta's proprietary setup may feel more opaque regarding how resources translate to performance improvements.

**Performance features**

Kinsta includes its global CDN as standard, automatic optimization of WordPress databases, and PHP workers that handle background processes efficiently. Cloudways offers solid performance fundamentals but treats premium features like dedicated support and advanced monitoring as add-ons. In our view, Kinsta's all-inclusive approach reduces decision fatigue, though it comes at a cost premium.

**Developer experience**

Both platforms offer SSH access, Git integration, and developer-friendly tools. Kinsta emphasizes its dashboard simplicity and premium support responsiveness. Cloudways appeals to developers who want transparency into cloud infrastructure and the ability to switch providers if needed.

## What happens next

Evaluate your priorities: if maximum performance and premium support matter most, Kinsta's proprietary infrastructure and included features justify the investment. If flexibility and cost-consciousness drive your decision, Cloudways' cloud-agnostic approach and competitive pricing deserve consideration. Test both platforms' free trials or starter tiers to assess how their dashboards and performance feel with your specific WordPress configuration.
