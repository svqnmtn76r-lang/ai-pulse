Anthropic announced it has received a US government export control directive mandating the suspension of all access to Fable 5 and Mythos 5—two of the AI firm's advanced large language models—to foreign nationals without exception. The practical implications are substantial for Anthropic's international operations and research collaborations, as well as for foreign organizations relying on these models for development and deployment purposes.
