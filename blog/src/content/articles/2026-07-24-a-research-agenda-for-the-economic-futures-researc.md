## What happened

Anthropic, the AI safety-focused company behind Claude, announced the creation of a $200 million Economic Futures Research Fund dedicated to supporting external research projects. According to [their official announcement](https://www.anthropic.com/news/economic-futures-research-fund-agenda), the initiative represents a strategic pivot toward understanding and influencing the economic implications of advanced AI systems.

The fund establishes a formal research agenda tackling critical questions about how artificial intelligence will affect labor markets, workforce transitions, productivity gains, and broader economic structures. Rather than conducting research internally, Anthropic is positioning itself as a patron of independent scholarship, funding university researchers, think tanks, and economic institutions.

This move comes amid intensifying scrutiny of AI's economic consequences. As companies deploy large language models across industries, policymakers and economists increasingly demand evidence about job displacement, wage inequality, and sectoral disruption. By funding external research now, Anthropic gains influence over how these narratives develop while maintaining plausible distance from the findings.

The announcement underscores a broader industry trend: major AI labs are investing heavily in governance, safety, and economic research to shape regulatory frameworks and public perception before government intervention accelerates.
