# Kinsta vs SiteGround: Managed hosting for WooCommerce stores

**Quick answer:** Kinsta and SiteGround both offer managed hosting tailored for WooCommerce, but differ in infrastructure approach, pricing structure, and target merchant size.

## Overview

Choosing a managed hosting provider is one of the most consequential decisions for WooCommerce store owners. The right host can mean the difference between a fast, reliable storefront and one plagued by downtime and performance issues. Two platforms frequently compared in this space are Kinsta and SiteGround—both premium-tier managed hosts with dedicated WooCommerce optimization.

This comparison matters because WooCommerce stores have specific hosting demands: they need reliable payment processing, fast checkout flows, robust security, and the ability to handle traffic spikes during sales. While both Kinsta and SiteGround recognize these needs, they take different philosophical and technical approaches to solving them.

## Key differences explained

**Infrastructure and performance:** Kinsta runs exclusively on Google Cloud Platform's premium tier, which in our view appeals to merchants who prioritize uptime and want their site backed by enterprise-grade infrastructure. SiteGround operates its own data centers, paired with a global CDN for content delivery. Both approaches are robust; the choice depends on whether you prefer the perceived security of a major cloud provider (Kinsta) or a company's direct infrastructure control (SiteGround).

**Pricing and scaling:** Kinsta charges per site with clear performance tiers—you select the plan matching your expected traffic and database size. SiteGround uses a more traditional hosting model with resource-based tiers and easier horizontal scaling as you grow. For merchants just starting out, SiteGround may appear more cost-accessible, while Kinsta's model better suits established stores expecting consistent, predictable traffic.

**Developer experience:** Both platforms offer staging environments, Git integration, and SSH access. Kinsta's focus skews technical—its dashboard and documentation assume WooCommerce developers comfortable with cloud concepts. SiteGround balances developer tools with a more accessible interface, which can benefit agency partners managing multiple client stores.

**Managed services:** Kinsta includes managed WordPress updates and automatic backups as standard. SiteGround similarly handles core updates and security scanning. Both reduce operational burden, though merchants should review the specifics of what "managed" means on each platform's documentation.

## What happens next

Evaluate your store's current traffic, growth projections, and team technical depth. If your team values cloud-native infrastructure and doesn't mind per-site billing, Kinsta's Google Cloud foundation warrants investigation. If you prefer traditional hosting with straightforward scaling and potentially lower starting costs, SiteGround deserves consideration.

We recommend testing both platforms' free trials or staging environments with a test WooCommerce store before committing. Check their current pricing pages and support documentation, as both providers frequently update offerings.
