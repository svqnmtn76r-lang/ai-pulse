## Key features

- **Topic Research and Content Gap Analysis**: Semrush lets you input a topic or keyword and instantly surfaces what questions your audience is asking, which angles competitors have covered, and which angles remain untapped. This feature matters because it shifts content planning from guesswork to evidence. You can see that while competitors have written about "email marketing automation," no one has thoroughly covered "email marketing automation for nonprofits"—a potential quick-win opportunity.

- **Content Template and Structure Recommendations**: The platform analyzes top-ranking articles for a given keyword and extracts their common structural elements—headings, word count, media usage, semantic keywords. Semrush then recommends a content blueprint based on what's actually working in search results. This prevents the common mistake of writing a 1,500-word guide when the top-ranking results are all 3,500+ words, or vice versa.

- **SEO Writing Assistant**: Built into the content creation workflow, this feature provides real-time feedback as you write. It flags readability issues, suggests keyword placement, and compares your draft against top-ranking pages for semantic similarity. The assistant helps ensure your content isn't just well-written—it's optimized for both human readers and search algorithms from the first draft, not as an afterthought.

- **Competitive Content Benchmarking**: Semrush lets you compare your published content directly against competitors' articles on the same topic. You can see which of your pages are underperforming relative to similar competitor content, and it suggests specific optimizations: add this keyword naturally, expand this section, or restructure your introduction to match user intent better. This turns content optimization from a manual, guesswork-heavy process into a data-driven checklist.
