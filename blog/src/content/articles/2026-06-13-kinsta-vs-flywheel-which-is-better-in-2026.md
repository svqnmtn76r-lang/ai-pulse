## Key differences

**Kinsta's approach** centers on raw performance and developer experience. The platform leverages Google Cloud infrastructure, which appeals to teams running mission-critical WordPress installations. Kinsta offers granular control through SSH access, WP-CLI support, and integrated Git workflows—features that resonate with developers who want flexibility. The platform's staging environments and straightforward database management make it particularly attractive for technical teams.

**Flywheel's philosophy** revolves around simplifying WordPress management for design agencies. The platform abstracts away infrastructure complexity, instead focusing on client management, billing features, and the workflows agencies actually use. Flywheel excels when you're juggling multiple client sites and need centralized billing and permissions.
