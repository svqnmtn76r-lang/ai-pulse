## What happened

Anthropic announced a substantial expansion of Project Glasswing, its initiative designed to advance AI safety research and development in underrepresented regions. The program is extending access to approximately 150 new organizations operating across more than 15 countries, according to [the company's official announcement](https://www.anthropic.com/news/expanding-project-glasswing).

Project Glasswing addresses a critical gap in the global AI ecosystem: the concentration of AI research, infrastructure, and expertise in wealthy Western nations. By providing resources, technical support, and collaborative opportunities to organizations in underserved regions, Anthropic aims to build more diverse perspectives into AI safety considerations and expand the talent pool contributing to responsible AI development.

The expansion signals Anthropic's recognition that sustainable AI progress requires international participation and that safety considerations benefit from globally distributed research efforts. This approach contrasts with the centralized model that has historically dominated the AI industry, where computational resources and research opportunities remain concentrated among a handful of major tech firms.

The initiative encompasses various forms of support—from technical infrastructure access to mentorship and funding mechanisms—tailored to organizations' specific needs. By reaching 150+ new organizations, Anthropic is substantially increasing the scope of this program beyond its initial pilot phase, indicating growing investment in international AI governance and safety research capacity building.
