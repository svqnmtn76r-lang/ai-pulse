The filing, which remained largely private at submission, represents standard procedure for large technology companies preparing for initial public offerings.

The move comes as Anthropic accelerates toward potential public markets entry, roughly four years after its founding by former OpenAI leaders Dario and Daniela Amodei. The company has raised significant capital—most recently a reported $5 billion funding round—and has established itself as a leading voice in responsible AI development.

By using the SEC's confidential submission process, Anthropic gains flexibility during the regulatory review phase without triggering public speculation about timing, valuation targets, or financial metrics. The confidential approach allows the company to iterate with SEC feedback privately before making any formal public filings or committing to a specific offering timeline.
