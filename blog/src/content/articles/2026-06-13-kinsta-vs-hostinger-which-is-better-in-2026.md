Hostinger provides adequate performance for typical shared hosting workloads, though resource contention can occur during peak usage.

**Specialization vs. versatility**

Kinsta's entire platform is built around managed WordPress hosting and modern applications. This deep specialization means features, documentation, and support staff are laser-focused on WordPress optimization, staging environments, and deployment workflows. Hostinger takes a broader approach, bundling shared hosting, VPS, domain registration, and a website builder under one roof. In our view, this makes Hostinger better for users wanting one-stop shopping, while Kinsta suits teams committed to WordPress excellence.

**Pricing structure**

Hostinger's entry-level shared hosting plans start at lower monthly costs, making it accessible for hobbyists and small projects. For cost-conscious developers, Hostinger's affordability is a significant advantage. **Developer experience**
