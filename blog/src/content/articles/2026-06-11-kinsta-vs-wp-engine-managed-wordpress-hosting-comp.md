## Performance

Kinsta is consistently rated the faster host, helped by Google Cloud's premium-tier network, Cloudflare Enterprise (included), and built-in APM for diagnosing slow queries. For sites where milliseconds affect conversion or Core Web Vitals, that speed edge typically justifies the small premium. WP Engine is fast and reliable too, with its own caching and CDN — strong, just not class-leading on raw speed in most tests.

## Support

WP Engine offers 24/7 phone and chat on all plans — valuable if you want to talk to a human during an incident. Kinsta offers 24/7 chat (no phone) but is frequently praised for the expertise and speed of its chat support. The right answer depends on whether phone support is a must-have for you.

## Dashboard and developer experience

Kinsta's custom MyKinsta dashboard is widely considered best-in-class: clean, fast, with staging, APM, and clear analytics. WP Engine's portal is capable and mature, with strong staging and developer workflows, but Kinsta usually wins on dashboard polish.

## Backups and data

WP Engine retains 60 days of backups versus Kinsta's 14–30 days — a point in WP Engine's favor for recovery headroom. Both offer automatic daily backups and one-click restore.

## Bandwidth and scaling

Kinsta includes unlimited bandwidth; WP Engine caps bandwidth by plan. For traffic-spiky or growing sites, Kinsta's unlimited bandwidth removes an overage worry.
