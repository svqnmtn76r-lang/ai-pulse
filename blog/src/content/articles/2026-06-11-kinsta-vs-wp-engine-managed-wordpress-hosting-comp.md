Managed WordPress hosting is where you pay a premium to never think about servers again, and Kinsta and WP Engine are the two names that dominate the bracket. They are close enough on the fundamentals — both fast, both reliable, both backed by serious support — that the decision comes down to a handful of specifics: how each meters bandwidth, how long it keeps your backups, whether you want a human on the phone, and how much the dashboard matters to your day. Get those four straight and the choice is clear.

The plan details below come from each provider's official pricing as of June 2026. Hosting plans and limits change; confirm the current numbers on each site before buying.

## The short version

Kinsta is the speed-and-polish pick — Google Cloud's premium network, Cloudflare Enterprise included, built-in application monitoring, and a dashboard most people consider best in class. WP Engine is the value-and-support pick — a lower entry price, 24/7 phone support alongside chat, and longer backup retention. Neither is a wrong answer; they optimize for different anxieties.

## Plans and specs

| | Kinsta | WP Engine |
|---|---|---|
| Entry plan | Single ~$35/mo (~$30/mo billed annually) | Essential Startup ~$30/mo annual (~$40 monthly) |
| Entry limits | 20GB server bandwidth, 10GB storage, 125GB CDN, 1 site | 25,000 visits/mo, 10GB storage, 75GB bandwidth, 1 site |

| Support | 24/7 chat | 24/7 phone + chat |
| Performance extras | Cloudflare Enterprise + built-in APM | CDN included |

At the entry tier the price gap is small — a few dollars a month — so this is a features decision, not a budget one. One correction worth flagging, because older reviews repeat it: Kinsta's entry plan does **not** include unlimited bandwidth. Like WP Engine, Kinsta meters it (20GB of server bandwidth plus 125GB of CDN bandwidth on the Single plan), so a traffic-spiky site should read the allowance and overage terms on the specific tier it's considering.

## Performance

Kinsta is the one most often measured as faster, and the ingredients explain why: Google Cloud's premium-tier network, Cloudflare Enterprise bundled at no extra cost, and built-in APM that lets you trace a slow query instead of guessing. For a store or landing page where load time moves conversions or Core Web Vitals, that edge typically earns back the small premium. WP Engine is no slouch — its own caching and CDN deliver fast, dependable results — it simply isn't class-leading on raw speed in most public tests.

## Support

WP Engine's clearest advantage is the phone. Every plan includes 24/7 phone and chat, which matters when a site is down and you'd rather talk to a person than type into a box. Kinsta is chat-only, but its chat support is repeatedly singled out for the expertise and speed of the engineers behind it. Whether that trade favors WP Engine depends entirely on whether phone access is a must-have for you.

## Dashboard and developer experience

This is where Kinsta tends to win on points. The MyKinsta dashboard is widely regarded as the cleanest in the category — fast, with staging, APM, and legible analytics in one place. WP Engine's portal is mature and capable, with solid staging and developer workflows, but on sheer polish Kinsta usually takes it.

## Backups

WP Engine retains backups longer than Kinsta's 14-day standard, which gives you more recovery headroom if a problem surfaces weeks after it was introduced. Both run automatic daily backups with one-click restore, so the difference is the length of the safety net rather than the mechanism. If long retention is important to you, confirm WP Engine's current window on its site, since the figure has shifted across plan revisions.

## Which fits your site

Speed-critical, conversion-sensitive sites lean Kinsta. WooCommerce stores can go either way — Kinsta for raw performance, WP Engine if budget or phone support weigh more. Agencies will be happy on either, choosing Kinsta for the dashboard and site management or WP Engine for the breadth of support. Tight budgets and a preference for phone help point to WP Engine.

## FAQ

### Is Kinsta or WP Engine faster?

Kinsta is generally measured as faster, helped by Cloudflare Enterprise and Google Cloud's premium network.

### Which is cheaper?

WP Engine has a slightly lower entry point; the entry-tier gap is small, so choose on features rather than a few dollars.

### Which has better support?

WP Engine offers 24/7 phone and chat; Kinsta is chat-only but highly rated for the expertise of its team.

### Which keeps backups longer?

WP Engine, versus Kinsta's 14-day standard retention. Confirm WP Engine's exact window on its site.

### Does either include a CDN?

Yes. Kinsta includes Cloudflare Enterprise; WP Engine includes its own CDN.

## The verdict

For most performance-sensitive businesses in 2026, Kinsta is the stronger managed WordPress host — fastest in class, best dashboard, with Cloudflare Enterprise and built-in monitoring included. WP Engine is the smarter pick when budget is tighter, when 24/7 phone support is non-negotiable, or when longer backup retention gives you peace of mind. Where speed drives revenue or rankings, Kinsta's premium usually pays for itself; everywhere else, WP Engine's value case is strong.
